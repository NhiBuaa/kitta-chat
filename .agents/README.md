## Compatibility And Retained Material

- `AGENTS.md`, `CONTEXT.md` and the former domain-rule paths are compatibility pointers
  to their current owners, without independent policy or glossary copies.
- `current-session.md`, `next-session.md`, `memory/last-session.md` and root
  `PROJECT-CONTEXT.md` remain historical references, not an active roadmap or authority.
- `events/`, `feature-delivery-events/`, `manual-tests/` and `workflows/` remain at
  established paths for historical evidence compatibility. Preserve locked bytes,
  approvals and append-only records; do not resume a workflow from their old commands.
- [Known issues](memory/known-issues.md) remain retrieval aids; verify against current
  code/tests before applying a past finding.

These retained locations are explicit exceptions to the default kit layout. New task
plans, handoffs, reviews and verification state belong in the relevant Issue/PR.
Do not create project-local commands, hooks or skills without a concrete approved need.
Personal/global skills are discovered from the session catalog and stay outside Git.
